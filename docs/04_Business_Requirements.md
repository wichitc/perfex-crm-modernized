# Business Requirements

## Purpose
Documents the business goals, workflows, and objectives that Perfex CRM and its modules fulfill.

## Scope
Leads, onboarding, invoicing, project management, accounting, HR, warehouse, recruitment, and WooCommerce sync.

## Detailed Explanation
Perfex CRM aims to simplify customer relationship management, sales pipelines, project operations, and financial auditing.

### Core Business Pillars
1. **Lead Conversion & Sales**: Capture visitor queries via Web-to-Lead forms, assign leads, track conversations, and convert them to customers.
2. **Invoicing & Billing**: Supports subscriptions, recurring payments, multiple currencies, tax codes, and automatic late fees.
3. **Project & Time Tracking**: Create client projects, log hours using timers, and bill tracked hours to the customer.
4. **Accounting Integration**: Synchronize all invoices, expenses, payments, and tax conversions into double-entry accounting records.
5. **HR & Recruitment**: Administer employee directory, work shifts, insurance, leaves, and recruitment pipelines.
6. **Warehouse & Stock Management**: Inventory track, delivery notes, goods receipt, internal transfers, and stock take audits.
7. **WooCommerce Sync**: Bridge e-commerce shops to CRM (sync orders, customers, and product catalogs).
